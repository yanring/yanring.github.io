MoE Parallel Folding

Dennis Liu*, Zijie Yan*, Xin Yao et al.

* Equal contribution. First authors listed alphabetically.

Introduces Parallel Folding, which decouples attention and MoE parallelism so each can be optimized independently. The framework achieves up to 49.3% MFU for Mixtral 8x22B on H100 and scales to 1,024 GPUs.
